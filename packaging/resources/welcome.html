QUAD-CAPTURE Driver for macOS

This package installs an open-source driver for the Roland QUAD-CAPTURE (UA-55) audio interface. Roland does not supply a driver for Apple Silicon Macs or for macOS 13 and later.

After the installation, macOS shows an audio device with the name QUAD-CAPTURE:

  • Inputs: IN 1, IN 2, DIGITAL IN L, DIGITAL IN R
  • Outputs: OUT 1L, OUT 2R (also PHONES), DIGITAL OUT L, DIGITAL OUT R
  • Sample rate: 44.1 kHz
  • MIDI IN and MIDI OUT: a MIDI device with the name QUAD-CAPTURE

CAUTION: At the end of the installation, the macOS audio and MIDI services restart. All audio on the Mac stops for a few seconds.
