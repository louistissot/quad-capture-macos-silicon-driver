The driver is installed

  1. Connect the QUAD-CAPTURE to the Mac with its USB cable.
  2. If macOS asks you to allow the accessory, click Allow.
  3. Open System Settings > Sound, and select QUAD-CAPTURE for the output or the input.
  4. In your music app, select QUAD-CAPTURE as the audio device.

To remove the driver, open the package "Uninstall QUAD-CAPTURE Driver".

To report a problem, open an issue on the project page on GitHub.
