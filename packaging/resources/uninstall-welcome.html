Uninstall the QUAD-CAPTURE Driver

This package removes the QUAD-CAPTURE drivers from this Mac. It removes /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL/QuadCapture.driver and /Library/Audio/MIDI Drivers/QuadCaptureMIDI.plugin.

CAUTION: At the end, the macOS audio and MIDI services restart. All audio on the Mac stops for a few seconds.
